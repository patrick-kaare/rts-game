# Feature ideas

Ordered roughly by how much each would improve play compared to the work involved.

## Controls
- **Control groups**: Ctrl+1–9 to save a selection, 1–9 to recall it.
- **Hotkeys**: S to stop, attack-move (A then click), and hotkeys for the build menu.
- **Edge scrolling and middle-mouse drag**.
- **Rally points**: right-click with a barracks or factory selected so new units go to that spot.
- **Waypoints**: Shift plus right-click to queue several moves.

## Economy and base building
- **Sell and repair** buildings.
- **Silos or storage limits**, so harvesting more ore isn't automatically always better.

## Units and buildings
- **Rocket infantry**: a cheap counter to tanks (riflemen only do 25% damage to vehicles).
- **Artillery**: long range, can't fire up close, strong against buildings.
- **Engineer**: captures or repairs buildings.
- **Tech building**: a radar or tech center that unlocks the minimap and higher-tier units.
- **Veterancy**: units get stronger as they get kills.

## Map and terrain
- **Neutral buildings** you can capture, like an oil derrick for income or a hospital that heals.
- **Map presets or a map editor** in addition to the generated ring maps.

## AI
- **Difficulty levels** (Easy, Normal, Hard), set by build speed and bonus income.
- **AI personalities** such as rusher, turtle and economy-focused.

## Multiplayer
- **Pings and map markers**.
- **Spectator mode**.
- **Replays**: the match log already exists, so this is mostly a playback viewer.

## Polish
- **Sound**: gunfire, explosions, unit responses, "construction complete" voice line.
- **Under-attack alerts**: a red ping on the minimap and a sound.
- **End-of-match stats**: units built and lost, credits harvested, a timeline.
- **Game speed setting** and pause for offline matches.

## Suggested first batch
Control groups, rally points, rocket infantry and AI difficulty levels.
